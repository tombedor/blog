# AI Output Scale Numbers

Useful figures for showing AI supply/volume. Treat these as mixed measures, not a unified census: removals, deliveries, generated images, and recommendation exposure measure different parts of the content pipeline.

## Music

- **Spotify:** removed **75M+ spam tracks** over the prior year; contemporary reporting put Spotify's catalog at **100M tracks**. Useful for "AI/spam output can rival the size of the retained catalog," but this is spam removals, not a clean AI-only count.
  - Source: Guardian coverage of Spotify's September 25, 2025 announcement: https://www.theguardian.com/music/2025/sep/25/spotify-removes-75m-spam-tracks-past-year-ai-increases-ability-make-fake-music
- **Deezer:** in January 2026, daily deliveries of AI-generated music averaged around **60,000 tracks**, about **39% of all music delivered every day**. Deezer also said up to **85%** of streams on AI-generated music were detected as fraudulent.
  - Source: Deezer FY2025 results, March 18, 2026: https://newsroom-deezer.com/2026/03/deezer-achieves-profitability-in-fy25-as-strategy-delivers-tangible-results/
- **Deezer earlier 2025 trend:** September 2025: **30,000+ fully AI-generated tracks/day**, **28%** of daily delivery; January 2025 was **10%**, April 2025 **18%**.
  - Source: Deezer Newsroom, September 11, 2025: https://newsroom-deezer.com/2025/09/28-fully-ai-generated-music/

## Images

- **ChatGPT / GPT-4o image generation:** OpenAI COO Brad Lightcap said **130M+ users** generated **700M+ images** in roughly the first week after GPT-4o image generation launched in ChatGPT on March 25, 2025.
  - Source: TechCrunch, April 3, 2025: https://techcrunch.com/2025/04/03/chatgpt-users-have-generated-over-700m-images-since-last-week-openai-says/
- **Older text-to-image baseline:** Everypixel estimated **15B+** AI-created images from 2022 through 2023 and an average of **34M images/day** since DALL-E 2 launched. This is older and explicitly estimated, but useful as a pre-GPT-4o baseline.
  - Source: Everypixel Journal: https://journal.everypixel.com/ai-image-statistics

## Video / Feed Content

- **YouTube recommendation exposure:** Kapwing/Guardian reported that **104 of the first 500 videos** recommended to a newly created YouTube account were AI slop; Kapwing also found **278** AI-only slop channels among 15,000 top channels, with **63B+ views** and **221M subscribers**.
  - Source: Guardian, December 27, 2025: https://www.theguardian.com/technology/2025/dec/27/more-than-20-of-videos-shown-to-new-youtube-users-are-ai-slop-study-finds
