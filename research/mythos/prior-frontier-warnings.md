# Source Notes: Prior Frontier-Lab Capability Warnings

## GPT-2 staged release, OpenAI, 2019

Sources: [initial post](https://openai.com/index/better-language-models/), [six-month follow-up](https://openai.com/index/gpt-2-6-month-follow-up/)

- Cleanest precedent for "alarming capability claim before / during staged release."
- OpenAI said it was not releasing the trained GPT-2 model because of concerns about malicious applications.
- It released smaller models first, then larger models over time, with partner research on misuse and detection.
- Follow-up says 774M was released after 124M and 355M, while 1558M remained subject to future release decisions.
- Dario Amodei and Daniela Amodei are listed on the original GPT-2 post; do not imply they personally drove the staged-release policy.

## GPT-4 system card, OpenAI, March 2023

Source: [GPT-4 system card](https://cdn.openai.com/papers/gpt-4-system-card.pdf)

- Launch-adjacent warning, not a withheld-release analogy.
- OpenAI warned about illicit advice, dual-use capabilities, and risky emergent behaviors.
- GPT-4 was trained by August 2022 and evaluated / red-teamed before March 2023 launch.
- Important caveat: ARC's autonomy evaluation concluded GPT-4 was probably not yet capable of autonomous replication and resource acquisition.

## o1 / o1-preview, OpenAI, September/December 2024

Source: [o1 system card](https://openai.com/index/openai-o1-system-card/)

- Launch-adjacent risk classification.
- OpenAI classified o1 pre-mitigation as overall **medium risk**, including **medium risk for persuasion and CBRN**, with low model-autonomy and cybersecurity risk.
- OpenAI said o1 did not sufficiently advance real-world vulnerability exploitation to meet the medium cyber-risk threshold.
- System card also disclosed Apollo evaluations for basic in-context scheming.

## Claude Opus 4, Anthropic, May 2025

Sources: [Claude 4 system card](https://assets.anthropic.com/m/6c940a1b69ed6a1c/original/Claude-4-System-Card.pdf), [TIME](https://time.com/7287806/anthropic-claude-4-opus-safety-bio-risk/)

- Strong Anthropic precedent for "we are releasing under stricter safeguards because dangerous capability thresholds may be near."
- Anthropic released Claude Opus 4 under **ASL-3** as a precaution, while saying it had not definitively determined that Opus 4 crossed the relevant CBRN threshold.
- TIME quotes Jared Kaplan saying the risk could include a novice terrorist gaining capability to make a more destructive weapon, while also saying Anthropic was not claiming certainty.

## Claude Sonnet 4.5, Anthropic, September 2025

Source: [announcement](https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-sonnet-4-5)

- Weaker analogy: released broadly, framed as "most aligned," but still under **ASL-3 protections** with CBRN classifiers.
- Shows the same safety-tier pattern had become normal for Anthropic's high-end launches by late 2025.

## Takeaway For Mythos

- GPT-2 is the closest match for "alarming claim plus restricted/staged release."
- Claude Opus 4 is the closest Anthropic-family precedent.
- GPT-4 and o1 are useful as "launch-time system-card alarm" examples, but they are not as close because they were not withheld in the same way.
