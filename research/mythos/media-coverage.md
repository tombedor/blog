# Source Notes: Media Coverage Of Mythos

## Axios: "Behind the Curtain: AI's scary phase"

Source: https://www.axios.com/2026/04/08/anthropic-mythos-model-ai-cyberattack-warning  
Date: April 8, 2026  
Authors: Jim VandeHei, Mike Allen

- Most rhetorically alarmed coverage found.
- Axios says officials believed Mythos could bring down a Fortune 100 company, disrupt internet infrastructure, or penetrate national-defense systems.
- Axios reports about **40 organizations** had access and frames Project Glasswing as a possible blueprint for future controlled releases.
- Useful pull quotes:
  - "AI's scary phase"
  - "isn't hyperbole"
  - "world-bending systems"
  - "superhuman powers"
- Use carefully: Axios's framing is much stronger than Anthropic's own more technical writeup.

## CNBC: Powell / Bessent / Bank CEO Meeting

Source: https://www.cnbc.com/2026/04/10/powell-bessent-us-bank-ceos-anthropic-mythos-ai-cyber.html  
Date: April 10, 2026  
Authors: Samantha Subin, Hugh Son

- CNBC confirmed that Federal Reserve chair **Jerome Powell** and Treasury secretary **Scott Bessent** met with major U.S. bank CEOs to discuss possible cyber risks from Mythos.
- CNBC says the bank executives were already in Washington for a Financial Services Forum board meeting when a special Tuesday meeting was called at Treasury.
- Bloomberg-reported attendees: Brian Moynihan, Jane Fraser, David Solomon, Ted Pick, Charlie Scharf. Jamie Dimon was reportedly the only major banking CEO unable to attend.
- CNBC says Bloomberg and the Financial Times first reported the meeting.
- CNBC says Anthropic briefed senior U.S. officials ahead of release on Mythos's "offensive and defensive cyber applications."
- Anthropic official told CNBC the company had been in "ongoing discussions" with CISA and CAISI about Mythos cyber capabilities.
- CNBC contextualizes the meeting with Anthropic's Pentagon blacklisting dispute and says cyber stocks had slumped after Fortune found an Anthropic draft blog post describing advanced cyber capabilities and risks.
- Useful pull quotes:
  - "urgent meeting"
  - "offensive and defensive cyber applications"
  - "ongoing discussions"
  - "cyber threat"

## Post-use Notes

- The media coverage strongly supports the "wonderful optics" point: the story moved from a product/safety launch to "Fed + Treasury + systemically important banks."
- The same coverage also supports a skepticism angle: the most alarming claims in Axios are sourced to briefed officials and framed dramatically, not to public reproducible evidence.
- Good phrasing: "Anthropic's technical disclosure was already alarming; the media cycle made it a financial-system stability story."
