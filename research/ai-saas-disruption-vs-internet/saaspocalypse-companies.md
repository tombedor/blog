## Threat Taxonomy

| Threat level | Why | Example companies |
|---|---|---|
| **High** — horizontal workflow | Seat-based, tasks AI can directly perform | Asana, Monday.com, Zendesk, HubSpot, Outreach |
| **Medium** — large horizontal CRM | Data moats + resources to adapt, but seat model exposed | Salesforce, Workday |
| **Adapting** — workflow + AI pivot | Embedding AI into outcomes, moving off per-seat | ServiceNow |
| **Lower** — infrastructure / vertical | Not mediating human labor; or compliance moat | Stripe, Snowflake, Twilio, Veeva |

**Zendesk:** Went private (Hellman & Friedman buyout, $10.2B, 2022) before SaaSpocalypse; last public price ~$77 vs ATH ~$170. Revenue growing (+24% to $4.05B in 2026) as private company, but still unprofitable. Cannot track market valuation; signals AI competition is forcing investment at the expense of margins.
  - https://getlatka.com/blog/zendesk-revenue/
