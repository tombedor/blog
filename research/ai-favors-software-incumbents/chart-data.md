# Source Note: Chart Data

## Airline Commissions

- Commission-rate points combine ARC/Travel Weekly reported average commission rates and major airline base-commission policy changes.
- 1994-1996 ARC average domestic commission rates: 10.05%, 9.17%, 8.57%.
- 1997: United cut base domestic/international commission to 8%.
- 1998: Travel Weekly reported average base rate had dwindled to 6.79% "last year."
- 1999: base commission cut to 5%.
- 2002: major airlines cut base commissions to zero.
- Sources:
  - https://www.travelweekly.com/Travel-News/Travel-Agent-Issues/ARC-Figures-Show-Sales-Up-Agencies-Down
  - https://www.travelweekly.com/Travel-News/Airline-News/TWA-matches-int-l-pay-caps
  - https://www.travelweekly.com/Travel-News/Airline-News/Carriers-save-nearly-3-36-B

  - https://www.wired.com/1998/03/name-your-price-airline-tickets/

## NYC Taxi / Uber Rides and Fares

- Data generated from Todd Schneider's dashboard API, which aggregates TLC monthly indicators and FHV base/trip reports.
- Rides chart: annual average trips per day, 2015-2019, yellow taxi vs. Uber.
- Fare chart: annual average passenger fare per trip, 2019-2025, yellow taxi vs. Uber. For Uber, monthly total passenger fare was converted to a daily value before dividing by trips per day.
- Sources:
  - https://toddwschneider.com/dashboards/nyc-taxi-ridehailing-uber-lyft-data/
  - https://taxi.toddwschneider.com/dashboard_data.json
  - https://www.nyc.gov/site/tlc/about/tlc-trip-record-data.page

## Online Brokerage

- E*TRADE daily average trades use quarterly values from an academic summary of E*TRADE financial disclosures; annual values are simple averages.
- Typical online commission points combine SEC, Internet News, Forbes, Wired, and E*TRADE pricing references; use as directional context for fee compression, not a market-wide weighted average.
