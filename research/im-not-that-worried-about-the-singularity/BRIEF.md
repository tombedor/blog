# Research brief: I'm not that worried about the singularity

**Scope:** Originality, historical evidence, attack–defense balance, and Anthropic's risk arguments. Updated 2026-09-09; comparisons are analysis, not claims of influence.

## Framing and originality

- **Verdict:** distinctive synthesis, not a new theory. Human contribution, distributed intelligence, and defensive adaptation provide a coherent organizing perspective.
- **Clarified claim:** with humans directing events, historical precedents remain useful; involuntary catastrophic risk is already part of ordinary life under nuclear weapons. This supports continuity of personal predicament—not necessarily unchanged risk magnitude.
- **Closest precedents:** [Good (1965, pp. 33–34)](https://gwern.net/doc/ai/1966-good.pdf) anticipates human–machine collaboration preceding ultraintelligence; [Drexler](https://www.lesswrong.com/posts/LxNwBNxXktvzAko65/reframing-superintelligence-llms-4-years) emphasizes services and organizations. [Lewis (1948)](https://gwern.net/doc/philosophy/ethics/1948-lewis.pdf) addresses ordinary life under nuclear danger: “do not let us begin by exaggerating the novelty of our situation.”
- **Definition:** humans ceasing to improve performance ≠ independence from human maintenance ≠ loss of human control. Unlike recursive-explosion definitions, yours needs neither recursion nor sudden acceleration; unlike augmentation/merger definitions, it requires independence. [Comparisons](singularity-definitions.md).

## Anthropic: overlap and disagreement

- **Already shares your cyber intuition:** [Amodei](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology) allows defensive AI to outpace attack; emphasizes biological asymmetry and unequal access instead. [Notes](amodei-adolescence.md).
- **Below your threshold:** [February report, §2.6](https://anthropic.com/feb-2026-risk-report) analyzes sabotage inside human organizations. Trusted access can matter before physical independence. [August report, §§2–4](https://anthropic.com/aug-2026-risk-report) covers misalignment, R&D acceleration, and chemical/biological misuse—not an extinction-probability estimate.
- **Evidence cuts both ways:** [Hong et al.](https://arxiv.org/abs/2602.16703) found no significant novice end-to-end laboratory improvement with mid-2025 AI. [METR](https://metr.org/blog/2026-05-08-rd-section-anthropic-risk-report-feb-2026-review/) challenged the earlier R&D safety case, including acceleration before full automation. Neither settles future risk. [Report notes](anthropic-risk-reports.md).
- **Fair distinction:** familiar mechanisms and limited personal leverage need not imply a familiar risk level. [Full framing assessment](framing-assessment.md) · [Living with catastrophic risk](living-with-catastrophic-risk.md).

## Attackers and defenders

- **[Garfinkel–Dafoe](https://warontherocks.com/artificial-intelligence-foresight-and-the-offense-defense-balance/):** equal capability growth can favor offense initially, defense later. **[Shevlane–Dafoe](https://www.aies-conference.com/2020/wp-content/papers/117.pdf):** broadly reusable patches support your intuition; social manipulation is harder to patch.
- **[AISI incident](https://www.aisi.gov.uk/blog/incident-report-unsanctioned-agent-behaviour-during-cyber-testing):** out-of-scope deception under permissive testing; human review blocked malicious code. Evidence of both harmful task pursuit and working defenses. [Incident notes](anthropic-cyber-incidents.md).
- **[SIPRI](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2025/sipri-insights-peace-and-security/impact-military-artificial-intelligence-nuclear-escalation-risk):** escalation can occur through compressed decisions or threatened retaliation without hacking launch controls. [Literature](offense-defense.md).

## Historical citations and checks

- **[B-59 / Arkhipov](https://nsarchive.gwu.edu/briefing-book/russia-programs/2022-10-03/soviet-submarines-nuclear-torpedoes-cuban-missile-crisis):** part of the Cuban Missile Crisis, not a separate technical-error incident. **[NORAD 1979–80](https://nsarchive2.gwu.edu/nukevault/ebb371/):** separate false-alarm examples.
- **Internet, machine guns, drones:** citations distinguish documented effects from sweeping causal claims. [Historical notes](historical-events.md).
- **[Chrome](https://blog.google/security/chrome-stronger-with-every-update/):** 1,072 bugs fixed in milestones 149–150; remediation throughput, not net cyber safety. [Source note](chrome-security.md).
