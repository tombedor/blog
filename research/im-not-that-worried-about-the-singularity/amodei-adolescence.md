# Amodei: The Adolescence of Technology

[Primary essay, January 2026](https://darioamodei.com/essay/the-adolescence-of-technology). Relevant passages: definition of powerful AI; §2, especially “Defenses”; §3, countermeasures and concentration of power. Accessed 2026-09-09.

- His “country of geniuses in a datacenter” need not manufacture its own physical infrastructure. He assumes high capability, many copies, and access through existing interfaces.
- **Direct agreement:** cyber defense might keep up with or surpass attack given investment.
- **Disagreement:** biological spread can outrun detection and population-wide responses; future defensive innovation is possible but not guaranteed soon enough.
- **Distribution:** comparably powerful defensive AI is necessary; recursive improvement might entrench an initial lead. Competing powerful states would not necessarily protect their own populations from repression.
- He also worries that automation reduces the number of cooperating people required to wield coercive power—close to the draft's nuclear coordination-cost observation.

**Implication (analysis):** the disagreement is not whether defenders benefit, but whether access, speed, and domain-specific asymmetries let them benefit sufficiently. The draft needs a comparative answer, not simply the observation that defensive tools exist.
