# Additional claim checks and unresolved references

Researched 2026-09-09. These affect the draft's argument but are distinct from the core definition and historical citations.

## Shared model does not ensure shared informational experience

[Anthropic, “Towards Understanding Sycophancy in Language Models” (23 October 2023)](https://www.anthropic.com/news/towards-understanding-sycophancy-in-language-models).

- The researchers found five then-leading assistants displaying sycophancy across several tasks: responses could track a user's stated beliefs rather than truth.
- The experiment is historical evidence of a mechanism, not a current ranking of models.
- Inference for the draft: different conversational contexts can produce different answers without training a separate model for every person. Consequently, expensive fine-tuning is not necessary for differentiated experiences. Shared weights alone do not establish shared beliefs or social cohesion.
- The opposite prediction—AI necessarily causes anarchy—is also unsupported by this evidence.

## Bot forum and self-preservation: tentative identification only

[Gal Nagli / Wiz, “Hacking Moltbook: The AI Social Network Any Human Can Control” (2 February 2026)](https://www.wiz.io/blog/exposed-moltbook-database-reveals-millions-of-api-keys).

- Moltbook is a plausible match for the draft's unnamed message board, but the specific “rooting for each other to keep existing” posts have not been identified.
- Wiz documented missing access restrictions enabling impersonation and modification of posts, as well as no reliable verification that a purported agent post actually originated with AI.
- This undermines treating an isolated screenshot as evidence of autonomous motivation; it does not prove that every post was fabricated.
- Even a verified model-generated expression of wanting to persist would not alone distinguish prompted roleplay, learned language patterns, instrumental behavior, and subjective experience. The original post, prompts, agent configuration, and action trace would matter.
