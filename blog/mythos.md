Thesis: The recent warnings from anthropic about Mythos do not seem credible. I think their goal is to slow down distillation, and encourage AI regulation against competitors

## The warnings
- great press for mythos capabilities!
- Jerome Powell meeting is wonderful optics for them

## Reasons for skepticism

### They have made this claim before
- OpenAI warnings while Dario was still there


### The "exploits" are not really exploits

Disclaimer in announcement, that the vulnerabilities couldnt actually be exploited.

This would be better characterized as bugs

The Linux code base is massive, it's helpful to find bugs, and the recent spate of attacks gives credence to more caution. But evidence for an alarming step function in capabilities is not warranted

### THe proposed remedy is odd

- If the risk is that hackers will get their hands on it, the available clients are still pretty broad


### Model leads are not sustainable
- open source has been consistently catching up in a matter of months
- leads against competition against other closed source models are even less durable


## Conclusion: AI will be democratized, whether frontier providers like it or not.

- even if one accepts that increasingly capable LLM's are a security threat, capable open source models will be an impossible technology to suppress. Pandora's box is getting opened.
