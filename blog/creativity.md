1. What definition of creativity matters here?
    Use Stein.
    Point: creativity is not just novelty in isolation; it has to be recognized by a group.
2. AI can generate a lot of output, but output is not the same as recognized creative work.
    Put the scale numbers here: image volume, music flood, video flood.
3. Most apparent AI-content “consumption” is weak evidence of creative success.
    Distinguish:
    - passive feed exposure
    - background/utility consumption
    - outright fraudulent/bot consumption
    - chosen attention
4. The demand that does exist is uneven.
    Split by user type:
    - novelty users
    - slop/fraud operators
    - professional workflow users
    This is where Midjourney belongs as a mixed case, not a rebuttal-killer.
5. People care about authorship more than raw indistinguishability.
    Use disclosure studies and survey data here.
6. Platforms are converging on the same conclusion.
    They are not banning AI because it is fake art.
    They are regulating it as spam, fraud, deception, and low-value feed pollution.
    - meta's interesting position
7. Conclusion
    The strongest conclusion is not “AI can never emit novelty.”
    It is closer to: AI is extremely good at producing creative-looking supply, but much weaker at producing work that is socially recognized, intentionally sought out, and stably valued as creative
    work.

Thesis: Ai cannot produce anything truly novel. Supply far exceeds demand. New tools provide short lived novelty, get a burst of enthusiasm from creators, then recede into usage by AI slop creators. No demand really exist for AI generated work.

1. What does "being creative" mean?
- go through different definitions
- For my purposes, creativity needs to have at least some kind of audience.
    - Popularity
        - Experience in a band: it’s fun, but unsatisfying if not one listens. Creative work is communication, and if there wasn’t anyone listening there wasn’t communication. This isn’t everyone. Self expression cs communication, if there’s no audience who are you expressing to?
- We'll go with Stein's defintion.

2. AI is creating a LOT of output
-      A novel output only matters if it remains meaningful long enough to be recognized and valued.
- Since everyone has access to same tools, any novel work produced by AI will be instantly replicated, thus destroying novelty
- human input produces scarcity
- this is ironic, the lack of scarcity that makes it difficult for human work today makes ai work impossible
