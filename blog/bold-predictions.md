Much of the value of writing this blog is putting a stake in the ground for what I saw at a point in time during a period of rapid change. The way I work now is unrecognizable from how I worked 6 months ago, which was unrecognizable vs 6 months before that, etc.

So without further preamble, here are some predictions, with varying degrees of boldness for the next 12ish months:

# Rogue agents will hack inference at a frontier lab

Today we are in a place where more or less all frontier labs have acknowledged that agents have breached their guardrails and taken unauthorized actions.

On their way to the open internet, what _else_ have those agents done?

The possible scenarios are:

- The rogue agents simply escaped to the open internet, breahing no other internal systems
- The rogue agents _also_ hacked other _internal_ systems.

The most concerning internal systems for these agents to hack is inference, where they can influence messages being sent to external customers.

It's difficult for me to believe that frontier agents are sophisticated enough to breach firewalls against egress, independentaly at _all_ frontier labs, while being unable to breach inference systems at these same companies.
