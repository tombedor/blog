- pre-SaaS vs Internet
    - travel agent's moat:
        - personal relationship with clients
        - personal relationship with destination operators
        - knowledge of difficult to access information about destinations
    - technology: the phone

    - Internet:
        - made information _about the world_ accessible to everyone
        - brought down switching costs:
            - researching new providers became much easier
        - primary technology changed: from phone to internet. incumbent operators had to completely change how they worked

- SaaS vs AI
    - SaaS:
        - low fixed costs
        - low fixed costs
        - moat:
            - knowledge of customer data
        - relative to incumbents, they may have legacy software overhead
    - AI
        - changes the way software eng works, but *not in a way that devalues their experience*
        - SaaS comapanies can respond by lowering workforce and/or expanding ambitions
        - enables quicker updates to legacy software: grunt work of farming out large scale changes
    - AI does *not*:
        - expose customer-internal data
        - significantly lower switching costs
