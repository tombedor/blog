- AI conforms to humans, not other way around
    - shifting standards
    - if AI is extremely capable, it shouldn't need very many dedicated instructions
    - help humans:
        - skeptics: good docs!
        - believers: helps AI
- Documentation heavy
    - in making a slackbot, the biggest gap was docs
    - AI can write docs well! review code, understand code, write docs
        - how do i know it's good?
            - same as i would any other collaborator
            - the cost of undocumented stuff is higher than small errors that can be quickly refined or corrected
            - it's always been like pulling teeth to get people to write docs
- Decentralized bets
    - people who are not on ai teams are left out
    - but lots of innovation will be from people in the trnecheds
- Reviews: NOT obsolete
    - emphasis on automated review:
        - code quality
        - linting
        - strongly typed languages
